**"Combination of searches for heavy spin-1 resonances using 139 fb<sup>&minus;1</sup> of proton-proton collision data at &radic;s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS detector"** is published in the *Journal of High Energy Physics*: [JHEP 04 (2024) 118](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP04%282024%29118). I am a co-author as a member of the ATLAS Collaboration. The preprint is [arXiv:2402.10607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10607).

The paper combines searches for new heavy spin-1 resonances that decay into pairs of W, Z, or Higgs bosons, or directly into leptons or quarks, using the full Run 2 dataset of 139 fb<sup>&minus;1</sup> collected in 2015–2018. Searches in quark pairs and third-generation leptons enter such a combination for the first time. In a heavy vector-boson triplet model, the combined data exclude masses below 5.8 TeV in a weakly coupled scenario and below 4.4 TeV in a strongly coupled one, constraints stronger than those from any individual analysis.
