I completed my M.Sc. in experimental high energy physics at [KEK](https://atlas.kek.jp/main/news/index.html). From April 2021 to September 2023 I was a junior physicist in the Inner Tracking Detector group of the [ATLAS experiment](https://atlas.cern/) at CERN. I worked on Higgs self-coupling physics in the HH &rarr; bb&tau;&tau; channel with custom CNN and GNN pipelines on ATLAS pixel-detector data, and with CERN on detector testing and data interpretation.

In September 2023 I started the Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering at [Tokyo Institute of Technology](https://www.isct.ac.jp/en) (now Institute of Science Tokyo), supervised by [Prof. Konstantinos Slavakis](http://www.slavakislab.ict.e.titech.ac.jp/).
