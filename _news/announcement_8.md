**"Overnight Stock Movement Prediction with Contextualized Embedding Incorporating News and Stock"** (with Junjie H. Xu, Liangcheng Lyu, and Yiming Bai) appears in the proceedings of the [2022 IEEE 11th Global Conference on Consumer Electronics (GCCE)](https://doi.org/10.1109/GCCE56475.2022.10014070), held in Osaka, Japan, on 18–21 October 2022, pp. 476–478.

Given a piece of news and a stock code, the model predicts the overnight movement of that stock from a combined news–stock embedding. One part is a contextualized embedding of the news, produced by a language model trained on news paired with the stock movements that followed it. The other is a stock embedding, produced by a pre-trained language model trained on information about the stocks. The paper also discusses what the stock embedding adds.
