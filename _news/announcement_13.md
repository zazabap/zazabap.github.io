I presented **"Background Estimation for Non-resonant Di-Higgs Production Search in the bbtautau Final State on the ATLAS experiment"** (with Tulin Varol, Yanlin Liu, Manabu Togawa, Yu Nakahama, and the ATLAS Japan Group) at the 2023 Spring Meeting of the Physical Society of Japan, held online on 22–25 March 2023 (talk 24aT1-6). The abstract is on [J-STAGE](https://doi.org/10.11316/jpsgaiyo.78.1.0_189).

The Higgs boson trilinear self-coupling &lambda;<sub>HHH</sub> sets the shape of the Higgs potential and can be measured through Higgs boson pair production. The bb&tau;&tau; final state is one of the most sensitive channels, but when the taus decay hadronically it is contaminated by multijet background. The talk presents a data-driven fake-factor estimate of that background, derived in control regions defined by b-tagging and the charge sign of the two taus, for use in new signal regions with better sensitivity to &lambda;<sub>HHH</sub>.
