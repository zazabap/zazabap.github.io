---
title: The paper on graph encoding with variational quantum circuit is accepted by IEEE QCNC 2025 in Nara, Japan.
date: 2025-04-01 15:59:00+0900
---

The paper on graph encoding with variational quantum circuits, [Tensor-Based Binary Graph Encoding for Variational Quantum Classifiers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14185), is accepted by IEEE QCNC 2025 in Nara, Japan. The details are on the [conference page](https://www.ieee-qcnc.org/2025/track1.php).
