**"Search for Nearly Mass-Degenerate Higgsinos Using Low-Momentum Mildly Displaced Tracks in *pp* Collisions at &radic;s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS Detector"** is published in *Physical Review Letters*: [Phys. Rev. Lett. 132, 221801 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.221801). I am a co-author as a member of the ATLAS Collaboration. The preprint is [arXiv:2401.14046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14046).

Higgsinos with masses near the electroweak scale could solve the hierarchy problem and provide a dark matter candidate, but they are hard to detect at the LHC when the charged and neutral states differ in mass by about 1 GeV. The search selects events with an energetic jet, missing transverse momentum, and a low-momentum track with a significant transverse impact parameter, in 140 fb<sup>&minus;1</sup> of proton-proton collisions at 13 TeV. For the first time since LEP, it excludes mass splittings between the lightest charged and neutral Higgsinos from 0.3 to 0.9 GeV at 95% confidence level, reaching Higgsino masses of about 170 GeV.
