I presented **"Performance Evaluation of Quality Control Data Acquisition System for Silicon Pixel Detector Production in ATLAS Experiment High-Luminosity LHC Upgrade"** (with Manabu Togawa, Koji Nakamura, Yoichi Ikegami, Kazunori Hanagaki, and the ATLAS Japan Silicon Group) at the 77th Annual Meeting of the Physical Society of Japan, held at Okayama University, Okayama University of Science, and online on 15–19 March 2022 (talk 16pA562-12). The abstract is on [J-STAGE](https://doi.org/10.11316/jpsgaiyo.77.1.0_154).

For the High-Luminosity LHC, ATLAS will replace its inner tracking detector, and every new silicon pixel module has to be threshold-tuned for the tracker to reconstruct tracks and calibrate energies well. The talk evaluates and improves the data acquisition system used for quality control in module production: the tuning algorithm, the charge-injection setup, and the choice of scans. The final evaluation of each newly assembled module combines its built-in charge-injection circuits, an X-ray source, and statistical tools in the local database.

At the same meeting I co-authored two more talks from the ATLAS Japan Silicon Group: S. Arakuta et al. on [the readout test system for the first prototype ATLAS pixel modules for the HL-LHC](https://doi.org/10.11316/jpsgaiyo.77.1.0_137) (16aA562-12), and K. Miyamoto et al. on [assembling the module cooling parts of the new silicon pixel detector](https://doi.org/10.11316/jpsgaiyo.77.1.0_173) (17aA561-5).
